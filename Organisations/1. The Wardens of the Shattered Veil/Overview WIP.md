Once, they were something else.

An ancient order of knights was cast into the ever-shifting expanse of [[The Mirror & The Veil|the Mirror]] during [[The Great Calamity|the Calamity]], severed from the world they once served. Their former name, their original vows, even the reason for their founding have eroded into obscurity—preserved only in dust-choked tomes sealed within the Order’s [[5.2. The Archive|Archive]]. What remains is the Wardens.

They endure within the ruins of their former keep, [[5. The Hearth|the Hearth]]: a crumbling bastion of stone and warding, anchored in a rare pocket of stability by ancient enchantments that weaken with every passing decade. The Hearth holds—for now.

The Wardens know their end is inevitable. Prolonged exposure to the _Mirror_ fractures flesh, thought, and memory alike. This slow, corruptive metamorphosis—_the Fracture_—cannot be halted, only delayed. Long ago, the Order abandoned its original oaths and replaced them with darker, harsher ones, choosing survival over purity while clinging desperately to the last remnants of their humanity. Their continued existence hinges on rigid [[1.1. Doctrinal Concepts|Tenets and Doctrinal Concepts]], enforced without exception.

No Warden is untouched by change. Some bear subtle marks; others are visibly altered beyond denial. None are fully human anymore.

Physical mutation is tolerated. Moral decay is not.

Any sign of ideological drift, loss of restraint, or risk of betrayal—whether in [[7. From Tribute to Warden - The Binding|Neophyte or veteran]]—is met with swift and merciless response. The [[8. The Quiet Watch|Quiet Watch]] passes judgment in many forms, up to and including execution by flame. Entire [[2. The Band|Bands]] have been erased when necessary.

These acts are not performed in cruelty, but in grim acceptance. The Order cannot afford mercy. No rank grants immunity; even the _Lord Commander_ stands beneath constant scrutiny. All will be judged in time. The cleansing fire waits patiently.

The Order **must** endure.

---

## Rooting Out Corruption and Weakness

The Wardens accept _Change_. They do not accept collapse.

While physical mutation is inevitable, corruption of the mind is not tolerated. Any Warden who violates doctrine, undermines cohesion, or threatens the Order’s stability—openly or in secret—is dealt with without hesitation.

Rank offers no protection. Neither _Unbound_ nor _Lord Commander_ are exempt from observation. Surveillance lies entirely with [[8. The Quiet Watch|the Quiet Conclave]], whose authority is absolute and unquestioned.

No Warden interferes with the Watch.

Those who do are remembered only briefly—if at all.
