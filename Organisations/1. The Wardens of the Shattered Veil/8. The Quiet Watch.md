# The Quiet Watch - Observation, Surveillance, Evaluation & Judgment

- The Watching
	- A covert observation assignment to a Warden by *The Silent Conclave*
	- Always masked as, and in conjunction with, a regular Succouring or a Stewardship transferral
	- Documents missteps, alarming behavior, tenant-oversteps, Unhallowed mutations in general
	- Files reports and delivers them to The Quiet Conclave

- The Watchers
	- Any eligible Warden temporarily assigned by The Quiet Conclave to conduct a Watching
	- Always anonymous, only The Silent Ones know who is currently Watcher
	- Having undergone past Evaluations prohibits a Warden from ever being a Watcher

- The Witnesses
	- Only in extremely rare cases, mostly when the Jury can not decide on a Judgement, the Quiet Conclave reveals the identity of the Wacher that filed a report
	- The Watcher then becomes a Witness and speaks before the Jury

- The Quiet Conclave
	- A collective of Mutes. Tasked with anonymizing Watch-reports, delivering them to the Jury and revealing Identity of the Judged only after a guilty charge is cast.
	- Also randomly chooses Bands to undergo a Watching, assigns Watchers and coordinates Watches with scheduled Succourings or Stewardships
	- Chooses and assigns Black Bonds to carry out the execution of Judgements
	- The Silent Ones are ritual components integral to the execution of the Watch system, not an independent body of power.

- The Jury
	- Seventeen randomly chosen Wardens that get summoned by the Quiet Conclave whenever a Watch has ended
	- Each Jury only meets once in the same constellation and only decides on one Judging
	- Any Warden eligible for duty as Watcher is also eligible as Judge

### The Judgements:

- The Marking
	- A Warden that has been evaluated and only a very minor infraction has been detected, the individual is considered Marked
	- Markings are always anonymous and only the Quiet track them to a name
	- The Warden in question is not informed
	- If a Marked gains a second Mark at any time, the sentence is automatically at least Parting

- The Dimming/The Unburdening/Declension
	- Only applied if a Warden shows signs of mental and/or deterioration that doesn't threaten the Order but makes them unfit to serve in the field (Physical Weakness, Intellectual decline, etc)
	- The Warden is stripped of rank, role and responsibilities and immediately transferred to the Hearth
	- They are *Withered* now, acting as servs from this point on

- The Parting
	- Social and structural severance for one specific Warden
	- Forced, permanent reassignment to another Band to sever unhealthy ties, prevent infighting and shatter budding rebellion

- The Sundering
	- Social and structural severance for an entire Band
	- The Band is permanently disbanded and its members are each assigned to a different Band

- The Shackling
	- Applied when a Warden manifests extreme mutation or instability that poses unacceptable risk, yet retains battlefield utility
	- The individual is stripped of all autonomy and held under permanent containment, now called *The Martyrs*
	- Release is authorized only under extraordinary circumstances and called *The Absolution*
	- Failure to recover the Shackled after deployment mandates immediate Erasure
	
- The Muting
	- The Warden is stripped of all his ranks, powers and identity
	- Tongue removal and partial lobotomy
	- The Muted serves in The Quiet for life

- The Erasure
	- Execution by fire. Can apply to individuals or entire Bands.
