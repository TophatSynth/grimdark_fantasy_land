# Overview:

An ancient order of Knights, thrust into the ever-shifting landscape of [[The Mirror & The Veil|The Mirror]] centuries ago, during the [[The Great Calamity|Calamity]], their former name and duties long since forgotten by the world and only remembered by dust covered tomes in the Order's [[5.2. The Archive|Archive]]. The Wardens still inhabit what is left of their former keep, [[5. The Hearth|the Hearth]], the crumbling structure anchored in relative stability by ancient, yet slowly waning wards. 

The Order clings to existence and self-preservation despite the inevitability of being consumed and changed beyond recognition by the twisting, corrupting nature of the *Mirror*, knows as *the Fracture*, in the end. This forced them to change, to evolve and to adapt their traditions long ago, forsaking their ancient oaths in exchange for darker ones while still clinging to the fading rest of humanity in them. They zealously rely on these [[1.1. Doctrinal Concepts|Tenets and Doctrinal Concepts]] as their existence hinges on them. 

Unable to fully resist the mutating effects of the *Mirror*, they heve long ago begun embracing, the inevitable physical and mental change. No Warden bears not at least some signs of mutation, some more overt than others but none is fully Human.

Occurring changes are closely monitored and members, not only [[7. From Tribute to Warden - The Binding|Neophytes and Acolytes]], that show signs of moral corruption, degradation and risk of turning on their brothers and sisters are swiftly, mercilessly and without fault dealt with, [[8. The Quiet Watch|punishments]] taking many forms up to execution by fire. 

These cullings of individuals and even entire [[2. The Band|Bands]] are not performed out of cruelty or callousness, but wccepted as necessary to ensure the Order's survival. Not only *Younglings* are subject to constant scrutiny, but even revered Wardens up until the *Lord Commander* are closely monitored by the collective. The cleansing flame waits for all those who fall from grace. The Ward **must** endure.

---
## The Order's purpose:

The Wardens primarily cling to existence and survival for two reasons:

- To fight the Mirror and its Horrors, protect the *Hearth*, safeguard the Order's legacy and ensure ten thousand years of battle, loss, hardship and suffering where not in vain.
- To do their part to protect Reality from the looming threat of destruction. They still see themselves as Custodians of Humanity, despite having been forced to forsake their ancient obligations long ago and often sn anything but by the ones they seek to shield against the darkness.

In practice, the Order pursues it's goals in a multitude of ways, including expanding their territory in the *Mirror* via [[6. The Tendrils (Name pending)|The Tendrils]], Eradicating mirrorborn Horrors with cold precisionand leading incursions into Reality.

All acts undertaken by the Order outside of the territory of their immediate control are collectively known as the [[4. The Hunt|Hunt]].

---
## The Order's Structure:

Structurally the Order is build upon shallow hierarchies throughout the rank and file Wardens, combined with democratically elected Leadership, extremely thorough vetting and indoctrination of potential recruits, and constant surveillance with the ever looming threat of *Erasure*

At the top of the Order stand the Lord Commander and Lord Librarian, elected by the collective and on duty until death or judged to be unfit to lead (most often [[8. The Quiet Watch#The Judgements|The Dimming]] due to age)

Below sit the High Wardens, of which there are only ever six, each one overseeing all Wardens of a particular [[2. The Band|Role]] and being elected by all Wardens within said Role.

Below the High Wardens come the regular Wardens, divided into individual [[2. The Band|Bands]] of five to eight individuals.
