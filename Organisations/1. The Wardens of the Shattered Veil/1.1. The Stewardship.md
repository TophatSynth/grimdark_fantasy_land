First and foremost: The Stewardship is **no** specialized role or position within [[Organisations/1. The Wardens of the Shattered Veil/1. Overview|The Order]]. 
Every [[2. The Band|Band]], as part of *the Transferring* is, as regularly as possible, rotated back to [[5. The Hearth|The Hearth]] to act as Stewards.

Whereas all *Bands* occasionally spend time at the Keep, the Stewards stay for an extended period of time. This happens due to the Order's usual doctrine of "Every Warden must be fit for every job" and the dire need to avoid any intermingling with non-wardens (exept for the Novices that are merely entrusted with the lowliest of work), but fist and foremost ensures ample opportunity for *Watchings* of the Leadership.

It is the Stewards that get, as randomly and unpredictably as with any other *Watchings*, tasked with surveiling the Lord Commander, Lord Librarian and High Wardens.

In addition to this important task, a Band's regular duties when on Stewardship include training Novices, acting as personal aide to the leadership, working at the [[5.1. The Vault|Vault]] and the [[5.2. The Archive|Archives]], sharing experiences and knowledge in [[The Great Hall]] and more.

The Stewardship also guarantees for a somewhat reliable, less stressed time to:

- Dedicate time to [[Organisations/1. The Wardens of the Shattered Veil/1. Overview#Severance and Unity - Internal Transfers, Routine Mechanisms & Social Control|The Self and The Joy]]
- Allow the Wardens' Bodies to recover from the *Fracture* build up in their bodies while out in the Wilds
- Partake in [[Organisations/1. The Wardens of the Shattered Veil/1. Overview#Severance and Unity - Internal Transfers, Routine Mechanisms & Social Control|The Bonding]] with other Bands in a more relaxed setting
