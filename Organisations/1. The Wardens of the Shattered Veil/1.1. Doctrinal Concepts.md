# The Tenets

These are at the very core of the Warden's existence. Forged and adapted over centuries they are a distillation of the contradicting foundation the Order is build upon. 
<br>

> [!Quote|author] Lord Librarian Duncan Farsight<br>127 AC
> "We may be Custodians of Humanity in a sea of Darkness, but we are also hunters. Predators fighting our way to the top"

<br>

##### I. Survival of the Order exceeds all. Our commitment to its continued existence is absolute. Brotherhood, honor, friendship and compassion, they all shall turn to dust before the Hearth falls to corruption.
<br>

##### II. Cherish your Kin, Trust them with your life. But never trust blindly. For tomorrow a Black Bond might come.
<br>

##### III. The Ward is your tribe, your Band is your Family. You will gladly die for both.
<br>

##### IV. Know your Kin like you know yourself. Celebrate with them, mourn the faded. Uphold the Memory.
<br>

##### V. Uphold the Ember that burns in your heart. For the Light shall guide your path, it shall shine bright in the face of the Horror. It shall flicker and die when the cleansing flames consume your soul.
<br>

##### VI. Embrace the Change, for it makes you strong. Watch out for Corruption, in yourself and in your Kin.
<br>

##### VII. The day and Black Bond comes for our Brothers and Sisters, or ourselves, we shall lay down our arms and repent. For it is on us to see the seeds of corruption taking root. For it is on us to cleanse the Order from its rot.
<br>

##### VIII. The Hunt is the Charge laid upon us, the path that was appointed. We shall not falter, for we are the true Custodians, standing between humanity and the horrors creeping in the dark.

## Severance and Unity - Internal Transfers, Routine Mechanisms & Social Control

- The Succouring
	- Temporary reassignment of Wardens between [[2. The Band|Bands]].
	- Used to bolster unit strength, Lend specific talents, share experience and similar reasons
	- Is always communicated to the [[8. The Quiet Watch|Silent Convlave]] in case a Watching is planned

- The Transferring
	- Periodic and mandatory reassignment of roles, routes, and routines of entire Bands
	- Prevents stagnation, ensures flexibility and reduces possible corruption

- The Bonding
	- Joint deployments of multiple Bands to reinforce cooperation, cohesion, cross-unit bonding and cross-observation.

- [[1.2. The Stewardship|The Stewardship]]
	- All *Bands* are regularly and rotatingly transferred to Stewardship as part of 
*the Transferring*
	- Not a punishment but part of the standard Transferring circle
	- Tasked with securing the Hearth, daily chores, assisting the Leadership

- [[1.3. The Joy & The Self|The Joy]]
	- Bands are actively encouraged to develop traditions, rituals and patterns that suit every member, strengthen unity and bolster morale
	- Wardens may openly show quirks and be individually distinctive
	- These freedoms end without comprise where they undermine Ward-traditions, Collective unity and invite corruption 

- [[1.3. The Joy & The Self|The Self]]
	- Wardens on a individual level may and should pursue goals, ambitions, passions that bring them satisfaction and validation. Most often these tend to manifest in the form of martial prowess, meditation and other obviously beneficial skills for the daily life in the mirror. But some Wardens also paint, draw, sculpt, write, debate, and many more activities that seem less beneficial. The true reason *The Self* is part of the doctrine is, that it keeps the Horrors of the mind at bay, grounds the Wardens in a glimpse of reality, of a life they will never know.
