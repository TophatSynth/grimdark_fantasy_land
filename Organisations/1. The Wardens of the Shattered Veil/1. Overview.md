# Overview:

An ancient Order of Knights, cast into the unreality, the ever-shifting landscape of [[The Mirror & The Veil|The Mirror]] aeons ago, during the [[The Great Calamity|Calamity]], forever severed from the world they once served. They have long since outlived their former name, purpose, duties and place in the world. Even the reasons for their founding have eroded into obscurity. What, and who they once were is remembered only in dust-covered tomes, sealed deep within the Order’s [[5.1. The Vault|Vault]]. What remains are *The Wardens of the Shattered Veil*.

What they are now is something different, something forged by centuries of survival within a realm that changes and twists. Corrupts flesh, mind, and humanity.

The Wardens still inhabit what is left of their ancestral keep, [[5. The Hearth|the Hearth]]. The crumbling structure anchored in a precarious pocket of relative stability by wards older than most civilizations and growing weaker with each passing decade. Beyond those wards, the Mirror presses inward. Fractured, patient and unrelenting.

The Order clings to existence and self-preservation in defiance of an end it knows cannot be avoided. Despite the inevitability of being consumed, being physically and mentally changed, reshaped and *fractured* beyond recognition by the corrupting nature of the *Mirror*, known as *The Fracture*, in the end. The Wardens understand this. This certainty forced them to evolve and to adapt, to alter their traditions long ago, forsaking their ancient oaths in exchange for darker vows, ones meant not to preserve purity, but to delay collapse, to combat decay, to preserve the Order above all else while still clinging to the remnants of their humanity, even as it inevitably fades away. 

These beliefs are the foundation of their [[1.1. Doctrinal Concepts|Tenets and Doctrinal Concepts]], which are enforced and relied upon with absolute zeal, as the Order's continued existence hinges upon them.

Unable to ever fully resist the mutating effects of the *Mirror*, no Warden is untouched by *The Change*. Some bear subtle signs, an altered gaze, an unnatural stillness, aura or skin-color, while others are incomprehensibly warped by mutation. None remain fully human. The Wardens do not deny this truth. They embrace it.

Within the infinite ways *the Fracture* can manifest, many are potentially useful, tolerated and even relied upon. Superhuman strength, speed, agility; insubstantiality; enhanced senses.

Yet these accepted changes seldom come alone.

Moral corruption and decay, loss of restraint, ideological drift, or any indication that a Warden may turn upon their brothers and sisters are all met with immediate, merciless and uncompromising consequences. These *Detrimental Changes* are closely monitored and judgments apply not only to [[7. From Tribute to Warden - The Binding|Neophytes and Acolytes]], but to all members of the Order without exception. [[8. The Quiet Watch#The Judgements|Punishments]] take many forms, up to and including execution by fire. 

These cullings of individuals and even entire [[2. The Band|Bands]] are not performed out of cruelty or callousness, but in grim acceptance. Every single member of the Order is subject to constant scrutiny. Even revered Wardens up until the *Lord Commander* are closely monitored by the collective through [[8. The Quiet Watch|The Silent Conclave]].

All may fall. All may burn.

The cleansing flame waits for those who fall from grace.

The Order **must** endure.

## The Order's purpose:

The Wardens cling to existence and survival for two primary reasons:

- To fight the Mirror and its Horrors, protect the *Hearth*, safeguard the Order's legacy and ensure ten thousand years of battle, loss, sacrifice and suffering were not in vain.
<br>
- To shield Reality itself from the looming threat of destruction and darkness. Though having been forced to forsake their ancient obligations long ago, the Wardens still regard themselves as custodians of humanity, despite often being feared, despised, demonized, hated and misunderstood by the ones they seek to protect.

Yet survival within the Mirror alone is impossible.

The Wardens, despite appearing barely human to those who lay eyes on them, still require the resources any human society needs. Food, materials, knowledge, and fresh initiates cannot reliably be found within the Mirror and must be taken from Reality.

Though forever severed from it, the Wardens retain the ability to cross the [[The Mirror & The Veil|Veil]] for short, dangerous intervals. These incursions serve to gather intelligence on the state of Reality, combat mirror-born Horrors and Veil-tears before they can spread, and extract tribute, in resources and lives, from communities placed under the Order’s protection.

To those they defend, the Wardens often appear as monsters. To those who defy them, they are.

In practice, the Order pursues its goals in a multitude of ways: expanding their territory within the *Mirror* via the [[6. The Tendrils (Name pending)|Tendrils]], eradicating mirror-born *Horrors* with cold precision, and leading incursions into Reality when doctrine or necessity demands it.

All acts undertaken by the Order outside of the territory of their immediate control are collectively known as the [[4. The Hunt|Hunt]].

## The Order's Structure:

Structurally the Order is built upon shallow hierarchies throughout the rank and file Wardens, paired with ruthless oversight and democratically elected Leadership. Recruitment is slow, vetting thorough, indoctrination exhaustive, and internal [[8. The Quiet Watch|surveillance]] absolute. The threat of *Erasure* is ever-present.

At the top of the Order stand the *Lord Commander*, the military and tactical leader, and the *Lord Librarian*, keeper of doctrine, lore, and spiritual cohesion. Both are elected by the collective and serve until death or *Judged* to be unfit to lead (most often [[8. The Quiet Watch#The Judgements|The Dimming]] due to age).

Beneath them sit the five *High Wardens* each overseeing a specific [[2. The Band|Role]] and elected by those who serve within it.

Where the above Leadership primarily remains within the [[5. The Hearth|Hearth]], the *Castellans* are stationed at and oversee the *Tendrils*, acting as commanders of *Bands* stationed there and responsible for their Tendril's security.

All Wardens not currently assigned to a leadership role are grouped into permanent [[2. The Band|Bands]] of five to eight individuals. *Bands* are led by the rotating role of *Bandmaster*

It is important to know, that **all** members of the Order are formally Wardens. Any of them could rise to be the next *Lord Commander* or *Lord Librarian* just as easily as the Leadership could fall to corruption and the cleansing flame tomorrow.

## How a Warden is Born:

The Order replenishes their ranks almost exclusively through the collection of humans from *Reality*. 

Some are given as *Tribute* by protected communities. Others are taken as *Spoils* from those who defied doctrine or openly resisted the Order.

Whatever the origin of such *Unbound* may be, their destiny is tied to the Order now, their former lives rendered irrelevant.

The [[7. From Tribute to Warden - The Binding|Path]] to becoming a Warden is long and brutal. Only few survive it.

If a prospect completes the grueling eight years of training, is assigned a [[2. The Band#Roles Within a Band|Role]] and survives *The Rebirth*, they finally earn the title of *Warden*. From this moment onward they are full members of the Order.

---
## How the Order roots out Corruption and Weakness:

In addition to the aforementioned way the Order deals with *Detrimental Mutations*, not only mirror-born corruption risks collapse. 

Any Warden who violates doctrine, undermines cohesion, threatens stability or endangers the Order in any other way, openly or in secret, is dealt with without hesitation.

Rank or accolades offer no protection. Neither *Unbound* nor *Lord Commander* are exempt from scrutiny and *Watchings*. Surveillance within the Order lays entirely in the hands of [[8. The Quiet Watch|The Silent Conclave]] and no Warden shall interfere with its absolute and unquestionable neutrality.

Those who are judged shall not be mourned, their memory only serving as a stark reminder of what corruption leads to.
