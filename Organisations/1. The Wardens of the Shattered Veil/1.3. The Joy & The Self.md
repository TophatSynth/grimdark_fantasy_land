Despite their bleak existence of constant fighting, dealing with the ever looming threat of Corruption, Ersure and Fading, the [[Organisations/1. The Wardens of the Shattered Veil/1. Overview|Wardens]] are actively encouraged to develop individual quirks, traditions, culture and interests.

- *The Self* focuses on the need of the humans, that all Wardens deep down still are, to express themselves individually, to discover passions and interests and to find solace in these as a coping mechanism against the hopeless nature of their existence. This can be on a per individual basis or as part of a [[2. The Band|Band's]] shared culture and traditions

- *The Joy* is the concept of allowing, and encouraging Wardens to deviate from the strict doctrine in moments of fragile peace, settings of inofficial nature or especially during stints at [[5. The Hearth|the Hearth]]. This includes but is not limited to: Reserving time to pursue aspects of *the Self*, celebrating camaraderie with fellow Wardens and Bands, etc

- As becomes clear, these two Doctrines are closely related and entwined. Both are permitted by Doctrine, albeit subject to close monitoring during *Watchings*
