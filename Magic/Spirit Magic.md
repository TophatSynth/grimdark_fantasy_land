# Overview
[[Spirits]] are beings of much power, being closely tied to [[The Veil]]. Thus, many practices have developed to harness that power to aid in survival in a harsh and unforgiving world. Being mostly (If not only) known and used by the [[tribes of the Northern Expanse]], Spirit Magic is an archaic and mystical practice, much more of an art than the rigorous constraints and rules of [[Veil-Scarring (Or Essence Weaving)|Essence Weaving]]. It generally can not be learnt. The ability to use it is, for most, innate — though it is unclear if it is a heritable trait. New users found to have the ability are therefore highly valued and given much training to develop their potential to its utmost. 

The general concept of Spirit Magic involves summoning a Spirit, then petitioning it for a fragment of its power or other service. This is a time consuming and laborious process at the best of times, however, with some Spirits being more inclined to leave [[The Spirit Realm]] than others, and even fewer are friendly enough to lend their power freely. 

Once obtained, though, the Spirit's power thrums powerfully within. Most often, it lends a keen edge to one's abilities, but in other cases, the effect is nothing short of supernatural. Either way, one must be careful to not be overcome by greed, lest they are consumed by it — Each blessing is, by nature, an aspect of the Veil, and the body can only withstand so much exposure.

## [[Spirit Communing]]:
- Simply said, one invites nearby [[Elder Spirits]] to their camp.
- The less civilized/tainted/tamed the wilderness is, the more likely one or in rare instances, multiple spirits show up. 
- Generally, being of elevated mind (Smoking mushrooms or herbs, consuming strong liquor, etc) greatly increases the chance for contact
- The more (non-medium) people are present, the less likely a contact is to happen
- These [[Elder Spirits]] are a weird bunch. They appear mostly sentient, albeit not comparable to Humans. They generally couldn't care much less about worldly issues. They also have a very different sense of time and scale/distance to worldly beings. What most of them share is a great sadness, as the Spirits are slowly fading. Most times a Medium will at most get some vague hints or statements that are very open for interpretation and a long winded (yet often pleasant) philosophical debate.
<br>
## [[Spirit Absorption]]:
- [[Spirit Fragments]], in contrast to [[Elder Spirits]] are generally not viewed as sentient, at least of not in any way we can identify.
- This practice sees the user absorb a wild spirit fragment into their body to gain specific benefits for a relatively short amount of time and with certain drawbacks.
