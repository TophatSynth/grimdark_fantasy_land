
Extract from "A Compendium of the Lands, Seas, History and Myth of the known World"  
Compiled and curated by Eddart of Gelthyr, wandering scribe and loremaster:

In a time long, long since forgotten, and turned to myth and hushed whispers, the world was ruled, shaped and dominated by the Empires of old, the most powerful societies the world has ever seen. Their relentless thirst for power and progress led to their undoing. Through the reckless use of magic, the art of Essence Weaving, they tore apart the Veil, the fragile barrier separating reality from the Mirror a dark, twisted reflection of the world. This event, known as [The Great Calamity, The Convergence, The Great Cataclysm and many other names](app://obsidian.md/The%20Great%20Calamity), unleashed chaotic forces that forever altered the course of history.

Now, ten-thousand years later, the world is but a shattered remnant of its former glory. The great ancient empires have crumbled, and Humanity is fractured into war-torn kingdoms, city-states, and fragile alliances, desperately clinging to survival in a world no longer fit for humans. Their great cities lie in decay, haunted by the echoes of the Convergence, places where the Veil remains thin, and magic warps the fabric of reality.

Amid the remnants of this broken world, twisted Horrors roam the lands, enabled to enter Reality by the areas where the Veil remains weak, or by careless humans, ignoring the lessons of the past and practicing _Essence Weaving_.

Human civilization and it's influence is almost entirely confined to heavily fortified towns and villages, even the smallest hamlets boasting at least a shallow moat and wooden palisade.

Farming and agriculture is only possible in the "Enlightened Zones" a few miles around settlements at most, areas deforested and heavily guarded and patrolled, but still only dared to be worked during daytime.

The wilderness between settlements is where the Horrors stalk, where supernatural and weird creatures roam, where death comes cheap and strange places loom.

The only respites are inns in various states of repair and fortification. They are situated 50 to 100 kilometers from each other along the crumbling remnants of the trade roads of old and offer rest, services and most importantly, bonfires to alarm the next inns of imminent danger.

The world is a grim, perilous place, filled with the scars of the Convergence and the ever-looming threat of the Mirror. Magic is unpredictable and dangerous, often twisting those who wield it, and the gods themselves, if they even exist, are of little help to ease the struggle and pain of humanity.

The Veil grows ever thinner. The Mirror's influence spreads. The world teeters on the edge of oblivion, and those who remain struggle to survive, ever haunted by the echoes of their past mistakes and the looming darkness of their future.

The Wardens of the Shattered Veil:

An ancient order of Knights, thrust into the ever-shifting landscape of The Mirror centuries ago, during the The Great Calamit, their former name and duties long since forgotten. They still inhabit what is left of their former keep, The Hearth the crumbling structure anchored in relative stability by ancient, yet slowly waning wards. 

The Order clings to existence and self-preservation despite the inevitability of being consumed and changed beyond recognition in the end. This forced them to adapt their traditions long ago, forsaking their ancient oaths in exchange for darker ones while still clinging to the fading rest of humanity in them. They zealously rely on these Tenets and Doctrines as their existence hinges on them. 

Physically they embrace, in very ritualized, controlled ways, the inevitable change. Slowly, in calculated doses to minimize risk, *Neophytes* are exposed to the mutating reality that is ever-present for the Order.

Occurring changes are closely monitored and members, not only proselytes, that show signs of moral corruption, degradation and risk of turning on their brothers and sisters are swiftly, mercilessly and without fault executed by fire. 

These cullings of individuals and even entire groups are not cruel, but necessary to ensure the conclaves's survival. Not only proselytes are subject to constant scrutiny, but even revered Hunters up until the Lord Commander are closely monitored by the collective. The cleansing flame waits for all those who fall from grace. The Ward must endure.

Centuries of exposure to *The Mirror* and embracing it's effects have, in addition to twisting and mutating them beyond recognition, taken an irreversible toll on the order: They are forever incapable of fully, permanently returning to whats left of Reality. 

Any incursion lead by the Ward are limited to a few days at most, the Wardens quickly weakening, fading, draining away. So, for better or worse, they are forever bound to accept *The Mirror* as their home.
