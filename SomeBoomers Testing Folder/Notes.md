*As the convoy snakes through the opening between the wreckage, a lanky figure slowly emerges from the underbrush beside the road.*

*His raised left hand slowly, shakingly drops to the side, the other clutching a duffle bag. His shoulder length, curly brown hair looks greasy, his eyes squinted nervously.*

*He is clad in scuffed blue jeans and sneakers, a faded bandshirt and a very worn olive green flannel. On his back is a black backpack with a hockey-stick strapped to the side.*


*He takes a slow, cautious step onto the pavement, hand stretched out towards towards the trucks, but as it becomes obvious that they are not stopping, he jerks around and disappears up the road*

*When looking back or in the rear mirrors, the people in the Convoy would see, just close enough to occasionally be disappearing behind bends in the road, a very beat up dark blue jeep.*

*It is just following, not making any attempt to get further attention, not honking, no high beams, just tired, squinted brown eyes and shaky hands on the steering wheel*


*As he sees Moore and Storm approach, his hands start shaking more, almost dropping the energy drink. His eyes dart from their faces to their guns and back*

*He nervously licks his lips, presses his eyes shut for two seconds as if to ground himself. Then, with a crooked smile, and still shaking hands he rolls down the window*
