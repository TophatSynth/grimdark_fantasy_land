# Corruption Gain

- 1 Corr per energy spend for magic (Spells are powers and cost 1 ER per 10 cp full cost)
- Certain enemies (not too uncommon) deal partial or full damage as Corr in addition to regular damage
- Learning certain skills (Hidden Lore for example) leads to 1 Corr per point spend
- Using corrupt items leads to 1d-3 Corr per use (No more than once per prolonged use)
- Environmental Corruption Level according to Environment:

| Type      | Level (ECL) | Corruption Gain                        |
| --------- | ----------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Clean     | 0           | No gain, Corruption slowly drains away |
| Tainted   | 1           | 1 corr pt per day                      |
| Blighted  | 2           | 1 corr pt per 12 hrs                   |
| Warped    | 3           | 1 corr pt per 6 hrs                    |
| Fractured | 4           | 1 corr pt per hr                       |

- Sleep in anything but *Clean* Zones: 1d-3 + CL Corr
- Submerged/Covered in Corrupt "Stuff": 1d-3 + CL Corr

---
# Corruption Loss

- While in *Clean* zones: Loose 1 Corr/day
- Meditation: Successful meditation-roll before sleep drains MOS extra
- Certain Rituals can cleanse

---
# Warding against Corruption

- Certain spells can increase resistance (Spells cause Corr though)
- Certain protective Items can absorb X amount of Corr
- Certain wards can lower environment CL

# Effects of Corruption

- Corruption-Threshold (CT) = 20 (CT 1 = 20, CT 2 = 40, etc)
- Roll against Will **or** HT at -1 per CT breached immediately whenever a *CT* is breached **and** at the end of each day if Personal Corruption Level (PCL) is above CT 1
- On success nothing happens and Corr-Level stays. Roll again next day
- On failure ***Mutation*** occurs.
- ==Roll **??What to roll here??**== to determine whether the mutation has ***Detrimental Side-Effects***
- **Luck** is available for Corruption checks
<br>

## Mutations: 
- Worth 1 CP per 10 Corr
- Physical for HT-roll, Mental for Will-roll
- Corr is used up for Mutations, fractions remain (On mutation at PCL = 46, 40 Corr are used up, 6 remain)
- Determined by GM + Player
- Must fit PC behavior, characteristics, flaws, traits, etc
- Can either introduce new traits or expand upon existing ones
- Mental Examples:
	- Combat Reflexes, Unfazable, Telepathic abilities, Increased Mental Attributes, etc
- Physical Examples:
	- Claws, Spines, Increased Physical Attributes, Damage Resistance, Wings, etc
<br>

## Detrimental Side Effects:
- Negative/detrimental/"evil"/etc traits in addition to mutations
- Physical for HT-roll, Mental for Will-roll
- Can either introduce new traits or expand upon existing ones
- New Traits always start at Quirk level
- Determined by GM (Player **can** weigh in with interesting ideas)
- Must fit PC behavior, characteristics, flaws, traits, etc
- Points in Detrimental Side Effects do **not** count towards disadvantage limit and do **not** grant any points.

# Humanity:

- Independent attribute starting at 10
- Can not be bought up or down
- Lowered by mutations and current threshold level
- Effects reactions, brings social/mental disadvantages when lowered
